From «قرب المسجد» to a verified national dataset

One engine turns descriptive Palestinian addresses and community road reports into verified locations — and every delivery it completes makes it more accurate. Two views: the pipeline, and the flywheel that funds it.

01

The engine — how one order flows

المحرّك: رحلة الطلب من نص وصفي إلى موقع مؤكّد
Live inputs & happy path AI & engines Fallback — once per address Execution & data asset

Reading it: all four input channels pass through the same two AI layers — extraction and entity resolution turn dialect text into structured facts; corroboration, anomaly detection and confidence scoring decide what to believe. The cascade tries the cheapest knowledge first (exact match → known building → landmark triangulation) and only asks the customer to pin when genuinely unsure — once, ever. Routing applies live checkpoint status as penalties, and every completed delivery lands in the graph already joined to the official cadastral record.

02

The flywheel — how it becomes a business

العجلة: كيف تتحوّل البيانات إلى خدمة تشترك فيها الشركات
Seed sources The asset What subscribers get Who pays & who feeds Growth loop

Reading it: free and official map sources prime the dataset; B2B subscribers consume it through one integration and their normal operations feed it back. Incentives are funded by prevented failed deliveries — a small discount costs less than one failed cash-on-delivery order — and rewards are only paid on verified outcomes, never raw submissions, so the data cannot be gamed. Every new subscriber makes the dataset better for all subscribers: a true data network effect.
